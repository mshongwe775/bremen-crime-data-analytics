# Embedding topic models
Original author: Aya Abdine (Member 04).

Both completed experiments use the supplied 768-dimensional vectors and the same 5,124 eligible reports. Title embeddings retain 59 topics, 4,583 assignments and 541 noise reports. Description embeddings retain 36 topics, 4,789 assignments and 335 noise reports. All original IDs and exclusions remain.

UMAP, HDBSCAN, BERTopic, c-TF-IDF settings, seeds, sensitivity experiments, sample IDs, keyword tables, measured historical runtimes and four original figures are retained. The 2D plots are separate visualizations of a five-dimensional clustering pipeline, not geographic maps.

Notebook 04 defaults to verifying and displaying saved results without retraining. Optional original fitting requires the recorded versions, especially PyNNDescent 0.6.0; the documented 0.7.0 trial changed clusters. Greater coverage does not establish greater accuracy. No independent human validation is claimed.
