# Time patterns and unusual increases
Original author: Ghofran Mahmoud (Ghod).

The analysis counts 5,124 eligible published reports from July 2019 to September 2026. Eleven months lack observations; boundary months are partial. Missing months remain missing. March 2021 is the largest monthly count (92).

Comparable seasonal profiles use 2021-2025. August averages 75.0 reports and December 58.2. Annual bands and reports-per-day profiles show exposure and variation.

The retained increase rule compares each month with its prior 12 consecutive observed interior months: baseline mean at least five and count at least max(10, 2*mean, mean+3*population SD). There are no citywide flags across 62 evaluable months. Seven category/district candidates and their source evidence are retained; none establishes a verified cause. Sparse series often lack a sufficient baseline.

`citywide_*.csv` holds citywide results; `time_results/` holds category/district counts, comparable annual and seasonal tables, increase screens and matching source reports. Categories are computational; no optional human approval is required. Publication selection, collection completeness, multiple comparisons and causal uncertainty remain limitations.
